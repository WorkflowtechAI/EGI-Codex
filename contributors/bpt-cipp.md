# BPT-CIPP

**Type:** Organization

## Why they matter to EGI

Publishes `rewst-actions-app`, a CIPP-adjacent actions app for Rewst. The CIPP community overlaps heavily with Rewst, and pre-built actions apps are useful scaffolding to avoid writing your own from scratch.

## Published content

### `BPT-CIPP/rewst-actions-app` (2024-09)
Electron-style app (package.json, main.js, preload.js, renderer.js, index.html). CIPP + Rewst actions app. Last pushed Sept 2024, so somewhat stale. UI styled with Montserrat and a dark theme.
