## URL migration

All nine posts were originally at `https://rewst.io/blog/<slug>` and are now at `https://rewst.io/success-stories/<slug>`. The old `/blog/` URLs 301 and then 404 on most titles. The new `/success-stories/` URLs all return 200. Google's index still shows the old `/blog/` paths for most results; follow the redirect or rewrite.

Two posts (Brandon Martinez and `rewst.io/blog/workflow-wizards-brandon-martinez/`) are still live at their original URL, suggesting the migration was imperfect.

## Patterns to steal

- **Document first, automate second.** Multiple practitioners (Brandon, Chris) build the doc before touching Rewst.
- **Modular sub-workflows as the unit of reuse.** Brandon, Brian, Chris, and Charlie all explicitly describe decomposing processes into reusable sub-workflows.
- **Replace psa migrations with integrations.** Brian's CW-Azure DevOps sync (140 hours) that avoided a $60k PSA migration is the class of economics to cite in positioning.
- **Emergency work as the gateway drug.** Alden built a same-day emergency ticketing system within 8 days of starting Rewst. Tight-deadline use cases get engineering buy-in faster.
- **AI-layer via OpenAI, not just Azure AI.** Brandon and Charlie both use OpenAI for content summarization and ticket classification rather than Microsoft cognitive services.
- **Customer portals over shared inboxes.** Gareth's portal handles ~90% of change-management tasks. Worth studying as an EGI client-portal reference.
